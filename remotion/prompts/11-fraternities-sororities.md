# 11 · Fraternities, Sororities & Student Organizations

**Composition:** `UC-11-fraternities-sororities-vertical` / `-square`
**Cast:** C6 Jules (chapter officer)
**Emotional arc:** every event gets its own vault; nothing lives forever in one thread.

Append negative blocks A–E to every generation.

**Casting note:** the Style Bible's 28–55 age guidance does not fit a student chapter, so
this pack runs younger — but it stays at the upper end of student age (21–24, seniors and
officers rather than freshmen) precisely because younger generations drift toward the
over-trained influencer look. Push hard on texture directives here; this is the pack most
likely to produce plastic skin.

**Compliance note:** no alcohol in frame, no recognisable Greek letters, no institution
names, no crests. Greek letters are letterforms and models render them as mush — they are
covered by negative block A, but call it out per shot as well.
