## Shot 1 · Cold open — `group-coldopen.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `cancun-beach.webp`

> A 30-year-old non-binary person with warm brown skin, short curly hair faded at the
> sides, a small silver nose stud, wearing an oversized open camp shirt over a tank, sits
> apart from a group on a sunlit balcony, head down, thumb-scrolling a phone held low and
> screen-away, while the group laughs out of focus behind them. Visible skin texture and
> natural asymmetry. Late afternoon side light, deep shadows, underexposed one stop. Shot
> on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 50mm at T2.2, slow push in, eye level. Candid, unaware of
> camera. Screen content never visible.

## Shot 2 · Escalation A · 4s

> Extreme close-up of a phone face-down on a bar top, buzzing continuously against
> condensation rings and bottle caps. Warm low evening light, shallow focus, deep shadow.
> ARRI Alexa 35, 85mm macro at T2.0, locked off.

## Shot 3 · Escalation B · 4s

> Nine pairs of hands reaching across a restaurant table at once, passing cash and cards
> toward the centre in a messy tangle. Faces out of frame entirely. Warm overhead pendant
> light, deep shadow. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 35mm at T2.4, gentle handheld, high angle
> looking down. Hands partly cropped by frame edges.

**Note:** hands are the highest-risk element in any generation — cropping them at the
frame edge and keeping them in motion is what makes this shot survivable.

## Shot 4 · The turn — `group-turn.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `group-cruise-deck-aerial.jpg`

> Wide aerial establishing shot of a cruise ship pool deck at golden hour, small groups of
> people scattered across loungers, the ocean stretching out behind. All figures are
> distant and small, faces not discernible. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 24mm at T2.8, very slow
> descending move. Warm gold highlights, restrained saturation.

## Shot 5 · Payoff — `group-payoff.mp4` · 8s

**Fallback:** `group-cruise-deck-aerial.jpg`

> The same 30-year-old non-binary person with warm brown skin and short curly faded hair,
> now in the middle of the group, head thrown back mid-laugh with an arm slung around a
> friend's shoulders, phone nowhere in sight. Golden hour, one stop brighter and warmer
> than the opening. Visible skin texture, clear catchlights, natural asymmetry. Others are
> background figures, softly out of focus, faces not discernible. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35,
> Cooke S7/i 35mm at T2.8, gentle handheld float, eye level. Candid.

**Continuity:** same reference still and seed as Shot 1. The phone being absent here is
the point of the shot.
