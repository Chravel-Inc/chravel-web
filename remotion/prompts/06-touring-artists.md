## Shot 4 · The turn — `tour-turn.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `coachella-festival-new.webp`

> Wide shot of a festival main stage at night from the back of the crowd, stage lights
> cutting through haze, thousands of distant silhouettes, no discernible faces. Shot on
> Sony VENICE 2, 35mm at T2.8, very slow push in. Warm stage light against deep blue
> night, restrained saturation, subtle halation on the light beams. No text or signage on
> the stage, screens, or banners anywhere in frame.

**Note:** festival stages are covered in LED walls and signage. State the exclusion
explicitly or the model will fill them with gibberish.
