## Shot 1 · Cold open — `wedding-coldopen.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `bali-wedding.webp`

> A 33-year-old woman with deep brown skin, hair in a low twisted updo with a few loose
> curls, soft natural makeup, visible skin texture and pores, wearing a silk robe, sits
> on the edge of a hotel bed in the early morning surrounded by garment bags and shoe
> boxes, holding a phone face-down against her forehead with her eyes closed. Soft cool
> window light from camera left, warm lamp fill behind. Underexposed by one stop. Shot on
> ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 50mm at T2.2, slow push in, eye level. Candid, unaware of
> camera. Phone screen never visible.

## Shot 3 · Escalation B · 4s

> clipboard paper is completely blank with no writing. Cool corridor light, deep shadows.

## Shot 4 · The turn — `wedding-turn.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `bali-destination-wedding.webp`

> Wide establishing shot of a beachside wedding arch draped in linen and flowers at golden
> hour, empty, with the ocean behind it and low sun flaring softly through the fabric. No
> people. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 24mm at T2.8, very slow dolly in, low eye
> level. Warm gold highlights, restrained saturation, deep shadows in the foreground sand.

## Shot 5 · Payoff — `wedding-payoff.mp4` · 8s

**Fallback:** `bali-destination-wedding.webp`

> The same 33-year-old woman with deep brown skin and a low twisted updo, now in a simple
> silk slip wedding dress with a delicate gold chain, and a 35-year-old man with fair
> freckled skin, tousled sandy hair and light stubble in a linen suit with the tie
> loosened, stand forehead to forehead laughing, eyes closed, at golden hour. Visible skin
> texture on both, subsurface scattering where the low sun rims their faces, clear
> catchlights. One stop brighter and warmer than the opening. Guests are background
> figures, softly out of focus, faces not discernible. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i
> 85mm at T2.0, gentle handheld float, eye level, 180-degree shutter. Candid.
