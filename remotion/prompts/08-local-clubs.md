## Shot 1 · Cold open — `clubs-coldopen.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `phoenix-golf-outing.webp`

> A 58-year-old woman with silver-grey hair in a short crop, deeply weathered sun-damaged
> skin, laugh lines, wearing a technical running jacket, stands alone at a park start line
> before dawn, breath visible, holding a phone face-down at her side and looking down an
> empty path. Visible skin texture and pores, strong and unglamorous. Cool blue pre-dawn

## Shot 5 · Payoff — `clubs-payoff.mp4` · 8s

**Fallback:** `phoenix-golf-outing.webp`

> The same 58-year-old woman with silver-grey cropped hair and weathered skin, in a
> technical running jacket, now mid-run in a loose pack of runners, laughing at something
> a runner beside her said, breath visible in the cold morning air. Golden low sun from
> camera left, one stop brighter and warmer than the opening. Visible skin texture, sun
> damage, clear catchlights, subsurface scattering where the sun rims her cheek. Other
> runners are background figures, softly out of focus, faces not discernible. Shot on ARRI
> Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 50mm at T2.4, gentle handheld tracking alongside, eye level,
> 180-degree shutter.
