# 09 · Faith & Church Groups

**Emotional arc:** lead the trip, not the group chat.

Append negative blocks A–E to every generation. Keep the treatment warm and plain —

## Shot 1 · Cold open — `faith-coldopen.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `faith-community-build.jpg`

> A 45-year-old Latino man with dark hair, wire glasses and a warm open face, wearing a
> plain t-shirt under a canvas work jacket with dust on his forearms, sits alone in an
> empty church hall late at night at a folding table stacked with blank paper forms, one
> hand over his eyes. All paper is completely blank — no printing, no writing, no text.
> Visible skin texture and pores. Single overhead strip light, cool and unflattering, deep
> falloff, underexposed one stop. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 35mm at T2.2, slow
> push in, eye level. Candid.

## Shot 2 · Escalation A · 4s

> Close-up of a clipboard holding a completely blank sign-up sheet with empty ruled lines
> and no writing whatsoever, a pen resting across it, on a folding table. Cool overhead
> light, shallow focus. ARRI Alexa 35, 85mm at T2.0, locked off.

## Shot 3 · Escalation B · 4s

> A phone face-down on a folding table buzzing repeatedly beside a set of van keys and a
> paper cup. Cool strip light, hard shadow. ARRI Alexa 35, 85mm macro at T2.0, locked off.

## Shot 4 · The turn — `faith-turn.mp4` · 6s

**Fallback:** `faith-community-build.jpg`

> Wide establishing shot of a half-framed timber house on a community build site at golden
> hour, scaffolding and stacked lumber, no people. Warm low sun through the frame casting
> long shadows, dust in the light. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 24mm at T2.8, slow dolly in.
> Restrained saturation, warm gold highlights.

## Shot 5 · Payoff — `faith-payoff.mp4` · 8s

**Fallback:** `faith-community-build.jpg`

> The same 45-year-old Latino man with dark hair and wire glasses, in a canvas work jacket
> with dust on his forearms and a hard hat, standing among a group of volunteers lifting a
> timber wall frame together, mid-effort and grinning. Golden hour, one stop brighter and
> warmer than the opening. Visible skin texture, sweat, clear catchlights, subsurface
> scattering where the sun rims his jaw. Other volunteers are background figures, softly
> out of focus, faces not discernible. Hands are gripping the frame and partly cropped by
> the frame edge. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke S7/i 35mm at T2.8, gentle handheld float,
> eye level, 180-degree shutter.

**Continuity:** same reference still and seed as Shot 1.
