# Character Bible

Fourteen recurring people. Every shot in every scenario pack casts from this list by ID.

**Why a fixed cast matters.** A face that shifts between the cold open and the payoff
reads as two different people and quietly destroys the film — the viewer will not name
the problem, they will just feel the footage is fake. Locking a small cast and reusing
each character's reference still (see `00-WORKFLOW.md`) is what buys continuity.

**How to use:** paste the character's **Prompt fragment** verbatim into the shot prompt.
Do not paraphrase between shots — the wording itself is part of what keeps the face
stable.

Ages skew 28–55 deliberately. Models generate that range far more convincingly than
18–24, which lands in over-trained influencer territory.

## Crowd and background people

For anyone not on this list, do **not** describe faces. Use:

> background figures softly out of focus, faces not discernible, natural candid movement

Every named face is a chance for the model to fail. Keep the named cast to the minimum
the shot needs, and let everyone else fall into bokeh.
